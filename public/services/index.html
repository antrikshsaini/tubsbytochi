FAQs

Bathtub refinishing involves restoring the surface of the bathtub to a like-new condition, typically by applying a new coating. Bathtub repair, on the other hand, focuses on fixing specific damages or issues, such as cracks or chips, without completely refinishing the entire bathtub.

The duration of the bathtub refinishing process depends on various factors, including the size of the bathtub and the extent of the work needed. On average, it takes about 3 to 5 hours for the refinishing process, but additional drying time is required before it can be used, usually within 24-48 hours.

Before our team arrives, please clear off your countertops of any items. Additionally, disconnect any appliances that may be attached to the countertops, such as stovetops or sinks. Our technicians will take care of the rest and ensure your space is protected during the refinishing process.

The cost of a kitchen or bathroom transformation varies widely based on factors like the scope of work, materials chosen, and the size of the space. We offer personalized quotes after evaluating your specific project needs to ensure accuracy and transparency.
