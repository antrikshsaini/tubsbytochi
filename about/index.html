About

TubsbyTochi

Welcome to Tubs by Tochi Incorporated, where we turn your kitchen and bathroom dreams into reality. With a passion for design, a commitment to excellence, and a dedication to customer satisfaction, we have been proudly serving Edmonton and its surrounding areas for the past four years.

Our Mission​

At Tubs by Tochi Incorporated, our mission is simple: to transform your spaces into stunning, functional, and environmentally conscious masterpieces. We believe that your kitchen and bathroom should not only reflect your unique style but also enhance your everyday life.

Why Choose us

Your Vision, Our Commitment

  • Expertise: Our team of skilled professionals boasts years of experience and a commitment to craftsmanship that sets us apart.
  • Eco-Friendly Practices: We're dedicated to minimizing our environmental footprint by using eco-friendly materials and sustainable practices.
  • Customer-Centric Approach: Your satisfaction is our top priority. We work closely with you from concept to completion to ensure your vision is realized.
  • Quality Assurance: Our work is backed by warranties, providing you with peace of mind about the longevity and durability of our transformations.

Tubs by Tochi Incorporated is more than just a business to me, it’s a labor of love and a commitment to excellence. For the past four years, our dedicated team has been on a mission to redefine beauty and functionality in your homes and businesses. Our journey is guided by a passion for design, a deep respect for our environment, and an unwavering commitment to your satisfaction. We are here to turn your dreams into reality, one countertop, one sink, one bathtub at a time. Your feedback and trust inspire us every day, and we are grateful for the opportunity to serve you.

Thank you for choosing us as your partners in transformation. We look forward to continuing this journey together.
